## Design Context

Context supplied by Databricks_Lake_Simulation_PRD.docx, version 1.0.

### Users

Platform engineers reviewing historical workspace coverage, data engineers following task inputs and outputs, and team leads explaining a fixed 24-hour replay.

### Brand Personality

Calm, precise, quietly playful. Operational truth takes precedence over decoration.

### Aesthetic Direction

An orthographic low-poly lake on a warm off-white operations surface, sage water, timber docks, folded white paper vessels, and restrained coral accents. Sora headings with Manrope body text. HTML labels remain readable over the scene.

### Design Principles

- The lake is the primary workspace; the list is an equally complete accessible alternative.
- Show state, evidence, and freshness separately.
- Preserve positions when filtering.
- Use motion to communicate elapsed estimated time, never measured data progress.
- Keep controls compact, readable, and keyboard accessible.

### Composition

Visual thesis: a miniature paper harbor in calm sage water, framed by precise operational controls.
Content plan: account navigation, lake with summary filters, contextual inspector, activity list and replay timeline.
Interaction thesis: constant-speed vessels and holding loops; quick selection feedback; camera focus, catalog docks, schema piers, and replay event navigation.
