# Canadian flag artwork

`canada.png` is a 1024 × 512 copy of the official National Flag of Canada PNG, preserving its proportions, colours, and maple leaf.

- [Canadian Heritage flag description and artwork downloads](https://www.canada.ca/en/canadian-heritage/services/flag-canada-description.html)
- [Official flag artwork archive](https://www.canada.ca/content/dam/pch/images/services/flag-canada/drapeaunational-nationalflagCanada.zip)

Retrieved October 9, 2026. Resized from the supplied 4800 × 2400 PNG for the lake scene's cloth texture.
